# Local SEO Plan: Gillette State Farm Agency

State Farm agents usually market through a **statefarm.com agent page** with limited editing, not a
fully independent website. So SEO here is mainly about **Google Business Profile** (the map pack),
your **agent page**, **reviews**, and **local links/mentions**. If State Farm permits an
agent-owned site through an approved vendor, Section 5 applies too.

**Objective:** rank top 3 in the Gillette map pack for "State Farm Gillette", "insurance agent
Gillette", "car insurance Gillette WY", and "home insurance Gillette WY" within 6 months.

## 1. What actually moves local rankings (in order of your control)

1. **Google Business Profile completeness and activity:** see the [GBP playbook](../local-presence/google-business-profile.md)
2. **Review count, rating, and recency:** see the [reviews playbook](../local-presence/reviews-playbook.md)
3. **Consistent name/address/phone** across directories: see the [citations checklist](../local-presence/citations-checklist.csv)
4. **Local links and mentions:** sponsorships, chamber, news, partners (Section 4)
5. **Your agent page** content and relevance (Section 2)
6. **Behavior signals:** calls, direction requests, and clicks from your listing (good photos and posts help)

## 2. Optimize your statefarm.com agent page

Complete everything State Farm allows you to edit:

- [ ] Professional photo of Todd and team photos
- [ ] Bio that mentions **Gillette, Campbell County, and Northeast Wyoming** naturally, plus years
      in the community, community involvement, and products you specialize in
- [ ] Every product line you offer, especially **ATV/off-road, RV, boat, snowmobile** (strong local demand)
- [ ] Languages spoken, hours, appointment options
- [ ] Community involvement section: teams, events, organizations
- [ ] Link GBP's website button to this page; link to it from Facebook and LinkedIn

## 3. Keywords people use in Gillette

See [`keyword-map.csv`](keyword-map.csv). Main themes:

- **Brand + location:** "state farm gillette wy", "state farm agent gillette", "state farm near me"
- **Product + location:** "car insurance gillette wy", "home insurance gillette", "renters insurance gillette"
- **Agent intent:** "insurance agent gillette wy", "insurance agency near me"
- **Toys:** "atv insurance wyoming", "rv insurance gillette", "snowmobile insurance wyoming"
- **Neighbor towns:** "insurance agent wright wy", "insurance moorcroft wy", "insurance sundance wy"

Use these phrases naturally in your agent page bio, GBP description/services/posts, and photo
captions. Never stuff them.

## 4. Local link and mention building (cost-effective)

Aim for 1–3 new local mentions/links per month:

1. **Gillette Chamber of Commerce** membership and directory listing
2. **School and youth sports sponsorships:** Campbell County HS, Thunder Basin HS, youth hockey/baseball (sponsor pages link out)
3. **CAM-PLEX events:** sponsorship of rodeo, fair, or expo events
4. **Campbell County Fair / 4-H:** livestock sale buyer or sponsor (very visible in ranching communities)
5. **Local press:** offer to be a source for the Gillette News Record or local radio on hail season,
   winter driving, or deer collisions (pre-approve messaging with State Farm)
6. **Partner pages:** realtors, lenders, and dealers who list "trusted local partners"
7. **Gillette College:** scholarship or career-fair participation
