# Referrals, Cross-Sell, and Retention

Your existing book is your cheapest growth channel. Moving a household from 1 policy to 2+ makes it
much more likely to renew, and each referral costs almost nothing compared with paid leads.

## 1. Cross-sell campaigns (email + phone)

Pull lists from your agency management system (AMS) each quarter:

| List | Offer | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-only clients who own a home | "Let's see if bundling saves you money" | Email, then call |
| Auto-only renters | Renters insurance (often a small add-on that can lower auto premium) | Email/text |
| Homeowners without umbrella | "$1M of extra liability protection for about the cost of a dinner out per month" (verify pricing) | Annual review |
| Home + auto with kids / mortgage, no life | Term life coverage review | Annual review |
| Business owners (personal lines only) | Business insurance review | Call |
| Personal lines clients who own a business | Personal lines review | Call |
| Clients with rental properties | Landlord policy review | Email |

**Cross-sell email template**
> Subject: {FirstName}, a quick idea that could lower your insurance bill
>
> Hi {FirstName},
>
> We take care of your auto insurance today. Many of our {CITY} clients save by placing home and auto
> together, and because we work with {N}+ companies, we can check that in about 10 minutes.
>
> Want me to run the numbers? Just reply "yes" or book a time here: {BOOKING_LINK}
>
> {Agent} | {AGENCY} | {PHONE}

## 2. Annual review program

- Contact every household **45–60 days before renewal** (call or email) for a 15-minute review
- Agenda: life changes (new car, teen driver, renovation, new business), coverage gaps, discounts,
  re-shopping if the rate increased more than about 10%
- Every review ends with: (1) a cross-sell question, (2) a review request if satisfied, (3) a referral ask

## 3. Referral program

**Offer:** thank-you gift card or local restaurant gift card for every referral *who requests a
quote* (not contingent on purchase).
⚠️ Many states limit referral rewards and prohibit rebating. Check your state's rules (often a small
cap, e.g. $25, and the reward may not depend on purchase). Confirm with your state DOI before launch.

**Referral launch email**
> Subject: Thank you, and a small favor
>
> Hi {FirstName},
>
> Most of our new clients come from people like you. If a friend, neighbor, or co-worker is
> frustrated with their insurance, we'd be glad to help them compare options, with no pressure.
>
> Just reply with their name, or send them to {DOMAIN}/refer. As a thank-you, we'll send you a
> {$X} gift card to {LOCAL BUSINESS} for each person who gets a quote.
>
> Thank you for trusting us,
> {Agent}

**Where to ask:** after binding, after claims, at annual reviews, in your email signature, on
policy delivery folders, and in a quarterly newsletter.

## 4. Strategic referral partners (B2B)

| Partner | Why they refer | What you give them |
|---|---|---|
| Realtors | Buyers need home insurance before closing | Fast binders, closing-day responsiveness, co-branded "new homeowner checklist" |
| Mortgage lenders / loan officers | Insurance holds up closings | Evidence of insurance within 24 hours |
| Auto dealers (independent) | Buyers need proof of insurance on the spot | Fast ID cards, after-hours contact |
| Property managers | Landlord clients and tenant renters policies | Portfolio reviews |
| CPAs / bookkeepers / attorneys | Small business and estate clients | Reciprocal referrals |
| Contractors / body shops / restoration companies | Claims situations | Reciprocal referrals |

**Goal:** meet two new partners per month (coffee or lunch, about $25), then stay in touch quarterly.
Track partner-sourced leads in your AMS.

## 5. Monthly email newsletter (simple, 1 hour/month)

- One seasonal tip (storm prep, winter driving, holiday party liability)
- One coverage explainer (water backup, umbrella, uninsured motorist)
- One local item (event you're sponsoring, client business spotlight)
- One CTA (book your review, refer a friend, leave a review)

Use a free or low-cost email tool (Mailchimp, MailerLite, or your AMS's built-in email).
