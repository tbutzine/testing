# Referrals, Cross-Sell, and Retention: Gillette

In a town of about 33,000, word of mouth and multi-line households are the cheapest growth you can
get. Each extra policy per household raises retention and revenue without new acquisition cost.

## 1. Cross-sell campaigns (quarterly; pull lists from your State Farm agency systems)

| List | Offer / angle | Best season |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-only homeowners | Home + auto bundle review | Year-round; hail angle in spring |
| Auto-only renters | Renters policy (affordable, and may help with multi-line discounts) | Year-round |
| Households with ATVs/campers/boats insured elsewhere (ask at reviews) | Bring the toys to one office | Mar–May, Sep–Nov |
| Homeowners without umbrella | Personal liability umbrella | Annual review |
| Families with kids/mortgage, no life policy | Life insurance review; shift and energy work make it urgent | Jan, and Life Insurance Awareness Month (Sept) |
| Clients with teen drivers coming of age | Teen driver discount programs (confirm availability in WY) | Summer |
| Clients who own a small business | Business owners policy / business auto review | Q1 |

## 2. Annual review program

- Contact every household **45–60 days before renewal**
- 15-minute agenda: life changes (new truck, teen driver, new toy, home project, new job), hail/roof
  deductible, liability limits (Wyoming minimums of 25/50/20 are low), discounts, life insurance
- End every review with: **(1)** one cross-sell question, **(2)** a review request if happy,
  **(3)** a referral ask: *"Who do you know who just moved to Gillette?"*

## 3. Client referral program

⚠️ **Check first.** Wyoming anti-rebating/inducement rules and State Farm policy govern referral
thank-yous. Typical safe practice: a small token of thanks for an *introduction* (not contingent on
purchase), within any dollar limit set by State Farm and Wyoming law, or a charitable donation per
referral to a Gillette cause. Confirm with your field leader before launching.

**Referral ask (approved wording)**
> "Most of our new clients come from people like you. If you know someone new to Gillette, or
> someone who just isn't happy with their insurance, we'd be glad to help them. Just have them
> mention your name."

**Community twist:** "Referrals for a Cause": for each referral this quarter, the agency donates
$X to {Campbell County food bank / youth sports / 4-H}. Share it in GBP posts and on Facebook
(if approved).

## 4. Referral partners (the Gillette new-mover pipeline)

| Partner | Why they refer | What you offer |
|---|---|---|
| **Realtors** | Buyers need home insurance before closing | Fast quotes and evidence of insurance; a "New to Gillette" checklist they can hand out |
| **Mortgage lenders / loan officers** | Insurance delays closings | Same-day binders and proof of insurance |
| **Apartment complexes / property managers** | Many leases require renters insurance | Quick renters policies; a flyer for welcome packets (approved) |
| **Auto & truck dealers** | Buyers need proof of insurance to drive off | Fast ID cards, after-hours contact |
| **Powersports, RV, and boat dealers** | ATV/RV/boat buyers | Quick quotes at point of sale |
| **Major employers' HR / relocation** (energy companies, Campbell County Health, school district) | Relocating employees | New-resident info session or flyer, if the employer allows |
| **Body shops / roofers / restoration** | Claims situations | Reciprocal referrals |
| **CPAs / attorneys** | Business and estate clients | Reciprocal referrals |

**Goal:** 2 new partner meetings per month; quarterly check-ins; track partner-sourced leads.
